Take the first step

How to Plant a Tree

Planting a tree is just the beginning. Caring for it is equally important.

01. Choose the Right Tree

Select a suitable native or locally appropriate species that can grow well in your area.

02. Find a Suitable Location

Consider sunlight, soil, available space, and the tree's expected mature size.

03. Prepare the Soil

Prepare a suitable planting area and follow proper planting practices.

04. Plant Your Seedling

Place the seedling carefully and cover its roots with soil.

05. Water and Protect

Give the young tree appropriate care and protect it from damage.

06. Watch It Grow

Monitor its development and continue caring for it as it matures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about trees and environmental protection.

Why are trees important to humans?

Trees help provide oxygen, shade, food, and other environmental benefits that support human life.

Can students participate in tree planting?

Yes. Students can join supervised planting activities and help care for seedlings in their communities.

How often should a newly planted tree be watered?

It depends on the tree species, soil, weather, and local conditions. Check the soil and follow appropriate care recommendations.

What happens if we continue losing trees?

Deforestation can contribute to habitat loss, soil erosion, disrupted water systems, and increased climate impacts.

How can I help if I cannot plant a tree?

You can protect existing trees, reduce waste, spread awareness, and support local environmental activities.
